# Code of conduct

Scientific Report Console is committed to a professional, inclusive, evidence-focused community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and critique ideas, evidence, and implementations rather than people;
- distinguish verified findings from hypotheses and uncertainty;
- protect private, restricted, unpublished, and personally identifying information;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks;
- accept correction when evidence or contract requirements change.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner or maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect contributors and users.

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
